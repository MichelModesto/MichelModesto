# Quintal do Paçoca: CloneCut prompts

Channel: **Quintal do Paçoca**, a Brazilian kids' channel (PT-BR songs). 3D animation like LiaChaCha / LooLoo Kids, with original characters, original songs and a Brazilian setting.
Cast: Nino (baby boy, 2), Lili (big sister, 5), Paçoca (caramel mutt puppy).

Rules of the routine:
- Every episode is 16:9 and 60-120 s. The 0-15 s hook is regenerated in 9:16 as the Short.
- On Sundays, post a 15-30 min compilation of the week. It needs no new generations, and long videos watched on TV are where kids' channels earn.
- Rotate the 7 formats (table at the end). YouTube's "inauthentic content" rule (Jul/2025) demonetizes videos made from one template with little variation.
- Always tick "Made for kids".

## 2. Template for every new episode

Copy Episode 1 and change only these blocks: the first line (title and duration), **Setting**, **MUSIC** (style of the song) and **SHOT LIST**. Keep **CHARACTERS**, **LOOK**, **SAFETY** and **DELIVERABLES** word for word. They are what keeps the channel recognizable.

A shot list that holds kids' attention follows these rules:
1. 0-3 s: a face close-up looking into the camera plus a striking sound. Never open with a logo.
2. A small visual surprise every 8-10 s.
3. The chorus repeats at least 3 times with the same choreography.
4. Call and response: a sung question, a 1-second pause looking at the camera, then the answer.
5. Imitable gestures: clap, jump, point, hide the face.
6. Always end with the sung sign-off "...no Quintal do Paçoca!" and Paçoca's bark. That line is the brand.

If Seedance garbles the Portuguese lyrics, make the song in Suno first with the lyrics locked and attach the audio.
